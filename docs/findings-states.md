# State EPR findings (checked 2026-11-16)

## Which states have laws
Seven: ME, OR, CO, CA, MN, MD, WA. No new packaging EPR law signed in 2026. Four trackers (SPC 2026-10-27, EPR Atlas 2026-11-15, Gypsum Association 2026-11-09, PPAI/Taft Nov 2026) agree; all secondary.
Status: fees_active OR, CO. fees_upcoming CA (full fees 2027-01-01; early fees billed mid-2026 per secondary sources), ME. registration_only MN, MD, WA.
Pending/stalled: NJ, IL, TN, MI, NC. Failed: NY, VT, CT, GA, NE (CT and VT 2026 laws cover bottles/cans only). RI and HI passed studies only. MA omnibus excludes packaging. NM, VA no bill.

## Surprises
- Oregon: enforcement paused Feb 2026 for one wholesaler group's members; judge upheld law late Aug 2026; on appeal.
- Colorado: two lawsuits pending, no injunction.
- Maine: no bidder for program operator; every deadline counts from a contract that does not exist, so key_dates empty. Statute threshold $2M (DEP page differs); flat fee under 15 tons ($500/ton, capped $7,500/yr per statute).
- Maryland exemption $2M or under 1 ton (fiscal note).

## Thresholds
- Oregon (DEQ confirmed): exempt if under $5M worldwide revenue OR under 1 metric ton (2,204.6 lb). Either test alone. Related companies aggregated. No application.
- Colorado: statute under $5M (inflation-adjusted each July 1) OR under 1 ton. Current figure not on any official page; JSON uses $5,779,297 (EPR Atlas, secondary); others say $5,506,200 or ~$5.6M. EcoEnclose: flat fee 10 tons or less, $800-$3,600. "$500,000" claim unconfirmed.
- California: $1M California gross sales only; must register and apply. Regs effective 2026-05-01; applications due 2026-06-01 (secondary); CAA plan filed 2026-10-13; fees and 10% plastic reduction 2027-01-01; 30% plastic recycling 2028-01-01.
- Candle jars: no agency guidance in any state. Suggest treating jar as likely covered glass packaging, labeled unconfirmed (our judgment).

## Environmental goals
CA: cut plastic packaging 25% by 2032; all recyclable or compostable by 2032; 65% plastic recycling by 2032; $500M/yr pollution fund from 2027. OR: plastic recycling 25% by 2028, 50% by 2040, 70% by 2050; life cycle studies; labeling task force. CO: recycling and recycled-content targets for 2030 and 2035. MN: all packaging refillable/reusable/recyclable/compostable by 2032; fees reward removing toxics. MD: recycling goals at 5/10/15 years; producers cover 50% of costs in 2028, 90% by 2030. WA: targets in CAA plan; reuse grants 2029; 90% cost coverage 2030. ME: reduce packaging and toxicity, no bans. Pending: NJ 50% plastic packaging cut in 10 years; NC PFAS.
