## Eco-modulation and bonuses
- Oregon: non-accepted materials pay higher fees. Bonus A 10% off base for life-cycle study (~8% net, cap $20,000). Bonus B 2x/2.5x/3x Bonus A for measured impact cuts (caps $40k/$45k/$50k). Bonus C reuse/refill (caps $40k/$50k). B and C claims were due May 31, 2026, credited on 2027 fees. Target: plastic packaging recycling 25% by 2028, 50% by 2040, 70% by 2050 (ORS 459A.926).
- Colorado: published dues include 5% surcharge for materials that disrupt recycling, surcharge outside core recyclable list, 5% discount for highly recycled. Four bonus forms (recycled content, reuse/refill, reduction, enhancement); amounts login-only. Statute requires 2030 and 2035 targets without percentages; secondary says 41% by 2030, up to 60% by 2035.
- California: rates include 5% discount for beating 2028 target and 5% surcharge for neither recyclable nor compostable. Proposed bonuses and virgin-plastic surcharge: secondary sources disagree. 2032 targets: 100% recyclable or compostable, 65% plastic recycling, 25% plastic reduction.

## Other states
Washington published a 2027 early fee schedule for 8 broad classes only (Paper/Fiber .005, Flexible Plastic .025); no file built. Minnesota fees begin 2029 (secondary). Maryland starts July 1, 2028 (secondary). Maine: no operator contracted.
