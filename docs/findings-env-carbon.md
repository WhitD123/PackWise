# Carbon findings (checked 2026-11-16)

Recommended family: EPA WARM v16 (Dec 2023) cradle-to-gate RMAM at WARM's 'current mix' recycled content for paper, glass and metals, paired with EPA GHG Emission Factors Hub 2025 Table 9 end-of-life factors (also WARM v16-based, no avoided-emission credits, landfill methane included, carbon storage excluded), so production and disposal can be added without double counting. Because WARM models plastics as resin only, plastic films use the UK DESNZ 'average plastic film' primary-production factor (includes product forming; 2023 dataset read via Climatiq), and PP film and laminate add the implied forming increment (0.576 kg/kg) to WARM resin values. EPS remains resin-only (ACC GPPS 2022) and is a lower bound.

## Recommended values (kg CO2e per kg; previous in brackets)
- corrugated: 0.937 [0.915]; end of life landfill 1.1023, recycling 0.1213, combustion 0.0551. WARM v16 current mix (35% recycled), US data and the same family as the end-of-life factors. DESNZ board (0.80) and FEFCO fossil cradle-to-grave (0.67, EU) are both somewhat lower, so the WARM value is a mildly conservative choice. Recycled content barely changes the WARM value (0.915 virgin, 0.937 current mix, ~0.98 recycled), so the default choice matters little.
- kraft-paper: 1.0913 [1.069]; end of life landfill 1.5543, recycling 0.022, combustion 0.0551. No US kraft-paper or paper-mailer factor was found in an opened source; WARM's own proxy for paper packaging (office paper) at current mix is used. DESNZ 'Paper and board: paper' value was not obtained (spreadsheet blocked). No peer-reviewed mailer LCA giving a per-kg factor was opened.
- paperboard: 1.0913 [1.069]; end of life landfill 1.5543, recycling 0.022, combustion 0.0551. WARM uses office paper as its proxy for folding cartons and paperboard packaging; current mix used for consistency with corrugated. DESNZ board (0.80) is ~27% lower, a real conflict between proxies; WARM kept for one consistent US family.
- molded-pulp: 1.0913 [1.069]; end of life landfill 1.5543, recycling 0.022, combustion 0.0551. No per-kg molded pulp factor found in any opened source. WARM office paper proxy (current mix) kept; mixed paper (1.27) is an alternative proxy. Treat as a low-confidence estimate; the TNO per-pack result suggests molded pulp may not beat EPS on carbon per insert.
- pe-film: 2.5603 [1.984]; end of life landfill 0.022, recycling 0.2315, combustion 3.0865. Only DESNZ includes product forming, so it is the only finished-film factor found. It is 29% above WARM's resin-only LDPE (1.984); the two US resin values (WARM 1.984, ACC 1.927) agree. The implied film-forming increment is 2.560 - 1.984 = 0.576 kg/kg (cross-dataset difference, not a measured adder).
- pp-film: 2.2516 [1.698]; end of life landfill 0.022, recycling 0.2205, combustion 3.0865. No finished PP-film factor was found (PlasticsEurope eco-profiles are only downloadable as zip files, which could not be read; DESNZ PP value not obtained). Resin value plus the same forming increment used for PE film keeps the two films comparable.
- plastic-laminate: 2.5933 [2.039]; end of life landfill 0.022, recycling 0.2425, combustion 2.5794. No laminate-specific factor found. The estimate (2.59) sits between DESNZ average plastic film (2.56) and DESNZ average plastics (3.17). It still omits lamination adhesive and any foil layer, so it is likely a lower bound.
- eps-foam: 2.917 [2.756]; end of life landfill 0.022, recycling 0.2425, combustion 3.329. Both values are polystyrene resin only; no EPS-specific or foam-expansion factor could be read (PlasticsEurope EPS eco-profile is only offered as a zip file). ACC GPPS 2022 is newer than WARM's PS data and 6% higher; treat it as a LOWER BOUND for EPS foam because pentane blowing agent and steam expansion are excluded.
- paper-plastic-mix: 1.6789 [1.435]; end of life landfill 0.9414, recycling None, combustion 1.2677. Weighted from the recommended kraft-paper and pe-film factors so it stays consistent with them. Bonding adhesive and the composite conversion step are not included.
- glass: 0.5842 [0.661]; end of life landfill 0.022, recycling 0.0551, combustion 0.011. WARM current mix. No second source was opened (Glass Packaging Institute LCA and DESNZ glass value not read in the time available).
- aluminum: 5.2911 [12.114]; end of life landfill 0.022, recycling 0.0661, combustion 0.011. WARM current mix (5.29) for family consistency; the Aluminum Association finished-can value (5.94, 73% recycled) is 12% higher and is a good cross-check. 100% virgin (12.11) would more than double the score, so the recycled-content default matters a lot for aluminum.
- steel: 3.34 [4.012]; end of life landfill 0.022, recycling 0.3527, combustion 0.011. WARM current mix. No second source opened (worldsteel tinplate LCI pages and DESNZ steel cans value not read).

## Cross-checks
- LDPE resin: WARM 1.984 vs American Chemistry Council 2020 1.927 (within 3%).
- Aluminum: Aluminum Association 2021 can study 5.935 at 73% recycled content, 12% above WARM.
- Board: DESNZ 0.802 (2023 via Climatiq, secondary) is about 15% below WARM corrugated; FEFCO 0.669 is cradle-to-grave fossil only.
- Laminate: DESNZ average plastics 3.172 is a broad upper bound vs our 2.593.
- EF Hub Table 9 spot-checked against the PDF: corrugated landfill 1.00, office paper 1.41, LDPE combusted 2.80, PS combusted 3.02 (MTCO2E per short ton), landfill excludes carbon storage and energy recovery.

## Caveats
- Paper landfill factor uses WARM office paper (1.554), WARM's proxy for paper packaging; it likely overstates methane from unbleached kraft. Corrugated (1.102) is the alternative; WARM net landfill with carbon storage is much lower (corrugated 0.198).
- EPS is resin-only (lower bound). PP film and laminate add a 0.576 forming increment estimated from DESNZ film minus WARM LDPE. Paper-plastic mix assumes 60/40.
- Glass and metals fell because the default moved from 100% virgin to typical recycled content.

## Could not confirm
DESNZ 2025/2026 spreadsheets (blocked); PlasticsEurope eco-profiles (zip only); finished-form factors for EPS, molded pulp, kraft mailers; measured film extrusion adder; paper/plastic split of bubble-lined mailers; GPI, worldsteel and peer-reviewed mailer LCAs; EF Hub recycling values for LDPE and PS (HDPE and mixed plastics used).
