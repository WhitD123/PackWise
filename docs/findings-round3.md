# Round 3 findings (checked 2026-11-16)

## USPS
- Ground Advantage Cubic still exists after July 12, 2026 (longest side limit raised from 18 to 22 in; soft-pack tiers cut to five). DMM 283 1.3 (spot-checked): commercial only; 1 cu ft or less, 20 lb or less, longest side 22 in or less; round each dimension DOWN to 1/4 in; L x W x H / 1728; lowest tier that fits (0.125 cu ft = tier 0.2). Soft packs: length + width, max 36 in, tiers 0.1 to 0.5.
- Notice 123 (effective 2026-10-04) p.15 weight prices and p.16 Cubic prices for zones 1-9; two reads agreed; Zone 9 = Zone 8. Zone 5 Cubic: 0.1 $9.04 ... 1.0 $18.68.
- Zones from 972 not filled: postcalc.usps.com/DomesticZoneChart needs a browser.
- Prices after Jan 17, 2027 not published; a weekly fuel surcharge is reported for 2027 (Supply Chain Dive, secondary).

## Packing rules
- UPS: at least 2 in of cushioning between contents and box; wrap fragile items separately; crumpled paper "ideal ... for lightweight, non-fragile items". FedEx: fragile items wrapped in at least 3 in of air-cellular cushioning; double-box with 3 in; outer box 6 in larger. USPS: no numbers (shake test).
- ISTA 3A (2018, Wayfair-hosted copy): under 70 lb, 18 in drops with one 36 in drop; small packages (10 lb or less, longest side 14 in or less) 24 in.
- UPS box strength chart: up to 30 lb 200 lb burst or 32 ECT; 31-50 lb 44 ECT; 51-65 lb 55 ECT ("only guidelines"). Uline agrees (secondary).
- Amazon FFP guidelines (2021): glass is fragile; 5 samples must pass ISTA 6-Amazon.
- Not found: numeric spacing between items; ISTA 6-Amazon heights; Rule 41/Item 222 size limits; USPS amounts; FedEx on air pillows.

## Candle jars
- "8 oz"/"12 oz" are jar capacities in fluid ounces (Uline, GlassNow, CandleScience).
- 8 oz demo: Uline S-17983M-GLD, 3 9/16 in tall, 2 7/8 in cap diameter, jar + cap 222.3 g, wax 175.8 g (CandleScience comparable), 0.877 lb.
- 12 oz demo: Uline S-22916M-GLD, 3 1/4 x 3 1/4 in, jar + cap 222.3 g, wax 241.0 g, 1.021 lb (1.135 lb if filled near brim).
- Flush Packaging F0190-1E (spot-checked): internal 6.06 x 5.63 x 3.56 in, external 7.44 x 6 x 3.81 in, 7.6 oz, $2.30 at 150, two-piece insert, lists Uline S-22916M-GLD as compatible. Five more Flush candle boxes added (1 to 4 jars).
- Not found: jar-sized molded pulp inserts with US price; standalone 2-4 jar dividers; US prices for honeycomb sleeves (excluded).

## EPR
- OR fee = rate x weight sold into Oregon (SB 582 sec. 11(2)); fees for year Y use Y-2 data (OAR 340-090-0700). CO likely the same lag (secondary).
- Producer of shipping packaging: OR "the person that packages and ships"; CO "packages or ships", two equally obligated producers for internet sales (rule 18.2.2(B)); CA no e-commerce clause (our reading: brand selling direct is producer).
- State assignment: CA counts delivery to a purchaser in CA; OR allows population proration as a fallback; use ship-to data.
- Exemption years: OR revenue most recent fiscal year, weight most recent calendar year; CO prior calendar year; CA most recent calendar year.
- Candle jars: no official ruling. CA lists lidded glass jars as covered; durable exclusion needs 5+ years; reusable exclusion needs a container designed and marketed for refill. Labeled likely covered.
- CA categories confirmed by CalRecycle (Sept 2025) except bubble wrap/stretch film (25_P15P) and loose peanuts (25_P47P). OR/CO definitions are portal-only.
- Colorado current threshold and flat-fee tiers: still not on an official page.
